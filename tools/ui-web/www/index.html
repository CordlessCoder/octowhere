OCTOWHERE / SIMULATOR

The firmware's screens, drawn by its own code and running in your browser. Touch the round screen as you would the device. The controls stand in for its sensors and keys, and every reading is synthetic.

POWERED OFF

Hold PWR to power it on

LOADING

  • Swipe sideways between the clock and the compass
  • Drag down from the top for settings, up to close them
  • Tap a setting to change it
  • Press PWR to rest the screen, hold it a second to power off
