# Baseline, supersession and exclusions

The implementation agent already has the 26 September complete backup and the 27 September charging/fault delta. The owner also personally validated the subsequent running UI. Keep the current firmware as the baseline for everything not explicitly replaced in `IMPLEMENTATION-DELTA.md`.

| Area | Current instruction | Superseded or excluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | V11 natural Maratype title, full-width subtitle, centered-hole pin and 1 s square dot; selected HOLD LEVEL horizon | Earlier G19 title/font, duplicate subtitle logo, upper L logo and faster dot |
| Clock composition | No clock wordmark; high seconds and wide lower well; retain currently implemented icon, scatter, rail and data | All OCTO/WHERE positions and slash proposal; old vertical battery column |
| Battery known/charging | Solid noncharging level; title-barcode-proportioned vertical slices in the wide well at charge hold | Old hatched fill, crawl, vertical V6 bands, outlined horizontal well, 13-slice horizontal study |
| Battery absent/unavailable | Static reversed 45° gray 15 px bands with 20 px gaps; `BAT --` remains truthful | Two gray dashes and 20 px down-right hatch trial |
| Fault screen | Keep the implemented 27 September V8 blue/yellow ticker, multi-fault summary and damaged exit | This package adds no new fault design or typography |
| Other Maratype trials | Keep existing type on self-test, settings, zone values, clock/compass NO DATA and every other shown context | The 29 September Maratype UI placement exploration was reviewed and rejected in full |

The agent's later implementation response records settled wider scatter on settings and clock, a 40% compass-background fade during exit, and an hour rail that opens from the center during clock entry. These validated changes are outside this delta. The old concept compositor in the selected clock study does not incorporate them; its unchanged pixels are **not** a request to restore an older scatter or rail.

The previous 27 September delta's vertical-gauge solid/segment wipe expressed the owner's accepted visual principle. This handoff places the bar horizontally. The V2 study did not produce a new transition animation at that geometry; the stated adaptation in the delta is an implementation integration instruction, not a newly owner-reviewed render. If the actual panel exposes a problem in that adaptation, capture it for design review instead of silently reverting to the old vertical gauge.

Source Marathon videos and firmware recordings are not repackaged. The owner will provide them if needed. Generated `identity-and-impact.mp4`, `startup-complete.mp4` and `clock-unoutlined-barcode-charge-loop.mp4` are concept review artifacts made from the supplied design sources, not source footage or evidence of firmware performance.
