## OCTOWHERE adapted identity: design timing, not source timecode

Current direction is `renderer/concept/startup-g19-matched/`; 466 × 466, 30 fps, four-second identity. Source videos above run on their own timelines. This table records approved beats and known frame detail; inspect the included GIF/script for precise positioning and remaining frame events.

| Beat | Project timing / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock | Lime grid's fixed-thickness tongues extend and join; blue `BOOT` turns clockwise 90°, so letter tops face right. Hard cut into the title. |
| Title appearance | Shapiro outline types on quickly; final word is centered at native proportions with microtext beneath. |
| Flicker | 2 frames on, 2 off/outline, 1 on, 2 off, 2 on, 2 partial vertical lines, then sustained. Small upper-right logo arrives about 14 frames later. Lime marks flicker. |
| Gray frame | Crosshair arms appear in a stagger; inner squares move horizontally outward, pause, then vertically outward. All pieces depart symmetrically. Their narrow gaps and corner alignment stay measured. |
| Plume | Two firmware-style 8 px-grid rectangle fields; upper field changes on identity frames 66, 77, 88, 99 and 110, lower field stays fixed. No scatter crosses rows 197–317; see matched README for geometry and surrogate-hash caveat. |
| Impact card | 19 frames total: 3 partial black-on-lime; 6 black-on-lime; 4 lime-on-black; 4 enlarged lime-on-black; 1 enlarged black-on-lime; final 1 black. Hard handoff to clock follows the startup sequence. |

Functional transitions elsewhere in the product follow their own millisecond holds and state changes. The 30 fps count describes ceremonial startup, not a universal screen animation policy.
