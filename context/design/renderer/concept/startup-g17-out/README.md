# OCTOWHERE G17 / unlocking grid entrance

466×466 motion study at 30 fps. These are concept renders, not firmware changes.

This replaces G16's entrance with an interpretation of the brief lime grid at approximately 0:17.27–0:17.53 in the supplied Marathon intro cinematic. Twelve solid blocks form a 3×4 grid on a dim blue field. Small voids open in a staggered order. Each tongue shifts right at constant height, visibly detaches from its cell, and finally meets the right neighbor. Vertical blue BOOT lettering sits beside the grid. The unlocked grid cuts to the fast outline type-on.

The gray marks use the corner vocabulary visible in the supplied official logo animation: fine crosshairs with diamond centers, detached squares, and short horizontal/vertical fragments. The later close-up supplied by the user was of our prior render; it identified an oversized diamond and fragments that lacked alignment. This revision reduces the diamond and registers both fragments on the axes of the square's corner facing the centre, each at the same narrow gap. The outer crosses now build with four distinct schedules instead of one shared outward slide: bottom-left horizontal first, top-right vertical first and late horizontal, bottom-right vertical then horizontal, and top-left vertical then late horizontal. The four squares appear together, hop outward horizontally over four frames, pause, then hop vertically over four frames, as in the logo clip around 1.20–1.85 seconds. The fragments and squares later clear in symmetric groups. BOOT is one word rotated 90° clockwise so the tops of its letters face right. The centered title, sparse purple scatter, lime framing crosses, reference-cadence flicker, delayed upper-right mark, logo-card impact, S1 self-test and F2 clock remain in the sequence.

The logo-card impact now occupies exactly 19 frames at 30 fps: 3 partial black-on-lime frames with vertical strokes through the logo silhouette, 6 full black-on-lime frames, 4 lime-on-black frames, 4 enlarged lime-on-black frames, 1 enlarged black-on-lime frame, and 1 fully black frame. The clock begins on its first visible entry frame after that black beat. The stage sizes and colours use the OCTOWHERE L1H mark.

The settings S1 sparse-scatter images are included for context.
